Fig. 01Profile

A physician from Istanbul, now training in internal medicine in Cambridge, with his sights set on a career in infectious diseases.

Dr Su graduated in medicine from Acıbadem University in 2021 and began specialty training in Infectious Diseases and Clinical Microbiology at Başakşehir Çam and Sakura City Hospital, one of Istanbul’s largest hospitals. He then passed the PLAB examinations, moved to the UK and joined the NHS, where he is now a Specialty Registrar in Internal Medicine at Cambridge University Hospitals.

His research spans fungal infections in transplant recipients, viral encephalitis, COVID-19 and malaria diagnosis.
