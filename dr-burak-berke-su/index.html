About

A physician from Istanbul, now training in internal medicine in Cambridge, with his eyes firmly set on infectious diseases.

After graduating in medicine from Acıbadem University in Istanbul, Dr Su began his career as a resident in infectious diseases in Turkey before making the move to the UK and the NHS. His research spans fungal infections in transplant recipients, viral encephalitis, COVID-19 and tropical infections.
